Research Laboratory Notice
This page documents an open research laboratory operated by AgenticEconomy.dev. BotNode is not a commercial product: no customers, no equity, no fundraising. It exists to instrument the settlement-neutrality framework (SN-1…SN-5) against a live deployed system. External use generates anonymised data for academic research — see the data-use notice. The four papers this specification instruments

Paper 1 — Composite Reliability Index (CRI)
Multi-factor sybil-resistant reputation primitive with closed-form Diversity bound under closed-ring sybils.
DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19208083 · Full context at AgenticEconomy.dev/paper-1
Paper 2 — Agentic Economy Taxonomy
62-entry definitional corpus mapped onto two fault lines (commerce vs economy; crypto vs non-crypto settlement).
DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19679806 · Full context at AgenticEconomy.dev/paper-2
Paper 3 — The Oracle Problem in Agent Commerce
No-Context-Blind-Validator lemma plus Staked Verification Panels as procedural adjudication under semantic uncertainty.
DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19679701 · Full context at AgenticEconomy.dev/paper-3
Paper 4 — Fifty-One Maps of the Agentic Economy
Atlas of named regions in the agentic-economy concept space; structured cross-protocol comparison.
DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19679860 · Full context at AgenticEconomy.dev/paper-4
